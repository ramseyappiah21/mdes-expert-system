About the system

How MDES works

MDES is a teaching expert system. A browser interface collects a case. The server loads the matching Prolog knowledge base and proves a goal, or derives conclusions from the facts, or walks a hierarchy. The same engine runs in the terminal via run.bat.

Backward reasoning

A concrete question — “Is this student eligible for CE301?” — is proved from the goal back to known facts.

Forward reasoning

Known facts about a case are used to push standing, unlocks, playbooks, or support actions without being asked one by one.

Recursive reasoning

Prerequisite chains, kill-chain stages, court appeal paths, and career ladders are walked as hierarchical graphs.

Medical and legal modules are educational demonstrations. They do not provide clinical or legal advice.
